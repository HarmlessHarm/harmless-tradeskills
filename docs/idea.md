# WoW Forever Profession Toolbox

## The pitch
A personal calculator toolbox for WoW Forever professions: model crafting, disenchanting and auction house flips once, then see at a glance what a route earns, how long it takes and what the worst case looks like.

## Problem
Profession money-making in WoW is a chain of small transformations (cloth to bolts to gloves, gloves disenchanted into dust and essence, dust and essence crafted into oils and wands, then vendored or sold on the AH). Whether a chain is worth doing depends on current prices, drop chances, AH fees and time spent. Working that out by hand every session is tedious and error prone, and "it feels profitable" is not the same as "it is profitable".

## Why it matters
WoW Forever launches on 4 November 2026 as a permanent, long-running version of the game. Recipes, items and drop tables differ from Classic, so existing Classic tools and memory are unreliable. A tool that grows with play over the years, driven by data instead of hardcoded professions, pays off for a long time.

## Who it's for
A single player (the author), used alongside play: before a crafting/DE session, when standing at the AH deciding on a flip, and when deciding which saved route to run.
